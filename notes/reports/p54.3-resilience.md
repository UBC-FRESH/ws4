# P54.3 spatial resilience metrics

Date: 2026-10-26
Issue: ws4 #378 (parent #375)
Branch: `feature/p54.3-resilience`

## Metrics

- **Connectivity** (levels, hectares/count): largest untreated component,
  percolation reach (`sum(area^2)/sum(area)`), component count, untreated area.
  Uses the full undirected block topology, not the directional spread account.
- **Age-class diversity** (levels, index/count): Shannon diversity, evenness, and
  occupied-class count of fragment area in 20-year classes.
- **Fragmentation** (flows, hectares/count): current-year opening patch count,
  mean patch area, largest patch area, and treated area. Reuses the existing
  patch metrics.

A block is untreated until a scheduled treatment is applied. Connectivity and age
are levels; fragmentation is a per-year flow. No metric enters the objective or
constraints.

## Pilot

`examples/resilience_pilot` uses the sample estate:

- Compact: blocks 1, 2, 9, 10 at offset 0. One opening patch, 42.9 ha.
- Scattered: blocks 1, 12, 25, 41 at offset 0. Four isolated patches, 38.6 ha
  total.

At offset 0, `resilience.fragmentation.patch_count` is 1 versus 4 and
`mean_patch_area` is larger for the compact schedule. `resilience.age_class.count`
is 5 for both, so fragmentation is not a restatement of age diversity.

Connectivity is correlated with removed area but not identical to the opening
pattern; age diversity responds to the simulated ages. The comparison shows all
three families separately rather than folding them into one score.

## Outputs

- `resilience_metrics.csv` (per year).
- `resilience_metadata.json` (schema, topology, age width, patch breaks,
  definitions, units, semantics, caveat).
- `report_resilience.csv` (baseline/final, total/mean/peak, unit, semantics,
  definition, caveat).
- `ws4 resilience compare` writes a two-run comparison with total/mean/peak and a
  same-direction flag.

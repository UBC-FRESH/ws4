# P54.2 structure habitat index

Date: 2026-10-26
Issue: ws4 #377 (parent #375)
Branch: `feature/p54.2-structure-habitat`

## Definition

The implementation follows Coops et al. (2016), *A forest structure habitat
index based on airborne laser scanning data*, Ecological Indicators 67:346-357,
doi:10.1016/j.ecolind.2016.02.057.

- Height: P95 of first returns (`height_p95_m`).
- Cover: percentage of first returns above 1.37 m (`cover_gt_1_37_pct`).
- Complexity: SD of first-return heights above 1.37 m
  (`complexity_sd_gt_1_37_m`).
- Breaks: height 10/20 m, cover 33/66%, complexity 3/5 m.
- The paper has **no weights**; it publishes a three-band class composite, not a
  weighted scalar. WS4 preserves that and does not invent weights.

A value exactly on a break is assigned to the lower class.

## Implementation

- `ws4 shi ingest` validates the three exact metrics and writes a stand table and
  versioned metadata (index version, citation, thresholds, boundary rule,
  weights statement).
- `ws4 shi diagnose` reports `index_computable`, present, missing, and proxies
  for a metrics table or a FEMIC `canopy_structure.json`.
- `ws4 simulate --structure-values/--structure-metadata` records
  `feature.SHI.height`, `.cover`, `.complexity`, `.area`, and class-area
  accounts. A treated block leaves the account for the rest of the run.
- `ws4 shi compare` joins structure components to DHI bands and reports n,
  Pearson's r, Spearman's rho, direction, and strength. No significance test.
- `ws4 report` writes `report_structure_habitat.csv` with the caveat.

## Pilot

The synthetic exact-metric fixture has four blocks:

| Block | Height | Cover | Complexity | Classes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 8.0 | 20.0 | 2.0 | low/low/low |
| 2 | 15.0 | 45.0 | 4.0 | medium/medium/medium |
| 3 | 25.0 | 70.0 | 6.0 | high/high/high |
| 4 | 35.0 | 90.0 | 8.0 | high/high/high |

The baseline keeps 40 ha with means 20.75 m, 56.25%, and 5.0 m. The harvest
schedule treats blocks 1 and 3; the surviving 20 ha has means 25.0 m, 67.5%, and
6.0 m. The fixture is synthetic and is not a management finding.

DHI comparison on the four common blocks reports negative rank agreement with
DHI cumulative for all three components (Spearman approximately -0.8), positive
agreement with DHI minimum (approximately +0.4), and negative agreement with
DHI variation (approximately -0.4). This is recorded as an exploratory result
with n=4, not as evidence about a real landscape.

## Real TFL 6 bundle

`ws4 shi diagnose --canopy-structure .../canopy__05__canopy_structure.json`
reports `index_computable: false`:

- missing: `height_p95_m`, `cover_gt_1_37_pct`,
  `complexity_sd_gt_1_37_m`;
- proxies: `chm_p95` (CHM-derived, not first-return P95),
  `chm_cover_ge_2` (2 m, not 1.37 m), and no first-return height SD.

The real bundle is not silently converted to the published index.

## Post-merge TFL 6 point-cloud follow-up

The LidarBC point cloud is publicly downloadable even though the FEMIC TFL 6
bundle did not materialize it. The real pilot is now in
`examples/structure_habitat_tfl6`: two OP24BMRS001 2024 LAZ tiles, 8 points/m²,
first returns normalized with the FEMIC DEM, seven VRI blocks. The exact index
is computable: mean height 22.95 m, cover 69.82%, complexity 6.86 m; five blocks
are H2-C2-K2, one H1-C0-K2, one H2-C0-K2. The earlier "not computable" result
applies to the bundle's CHM-only product list, not to data access.
